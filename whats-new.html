What's new

Walked through everything that's new so you and the guys can put it to work:

You can actually see a site now without driving to it.

On any spot in the list, there's a one-click button that drops you right on the corner in Google Street View. There's also a satellite view of the map, so you can flip from streets to aerial and eyeball the actual parking lot, patio room, how you'd get in and out. Basically scout the pad from your couch.

It sizes up the competition for you.

Every site now pulls live from Google and shows you the nearest Cava, Qdoba, Sweetgreen and Chipotle, plus whether there's an In-N-Out or Chick-fil-A close by, and the gyms nearby (fitness folks eat bowls, so that's a good signal). And when a spot has no bowl competition within a mile and a half, it literally flags it as open field, which is exactly what you want to see. You can also flip pins on and off for any of those right on the map to scan a whole area at once.

Adding a new city or a new person is way easier.

There's an "Admin" button up top now. You type in the city and state, paste the broker's Google Sheet link, and it sets up the new market. No more hand-wiring the thing every time you add somebody (that was the part eating up your tokens the other day).

And a pile of little stuff that adds up:

  • Pulled in all the Austin sites that were missing before.
  • Cleaned up the gym list so it's real gyms, not hotel workout rooms.
  • Added a "Refresh" button so if the live data hiccups, you can re-pull it in one click.
  • Pointed the little "something's not loading" help button at me, so if it ever acts up your guys just text me for tech support instead of bugging you.
  • Tidied up the busy top of the page (there's a "Fit details" button to hide or show the scoring key whenever you want).
  • Fixed it so it actually works on your phone. It was getting cut off before, which matters since you're usually looking at this from the road.
It's all live on your dashboard now, and everything on your old version is revertable with a click. If anything ever feels off, just text me and I'll flip it right back.
