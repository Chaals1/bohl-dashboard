What's new

The big one

Real competitor intel on every single site

Before
The only competitor info was a static list of Chipotles you'd plotted onto a map one time, by hand.
Now
Every one of your 50+ sites pulls real data straight from Google and scores the competition around it, automatically.
  • Shows the nearest Cava, Qdoba, Sweetgreen and Chipotle — your actual competition, not just Chipotle.
  • Tells you if an In-N-Out or Chick-fil-A is close by (the traffic magnets you want as neighbors).
  • Counts the gyms nearby — those folks walk out hungry for exactly what you sell.
  • Gives you the exact distance to each one, per site.
  • Calls out the single nearest competitor by name right at the top of each site — "Chipotle Mexican Grill, 0.69 mi" — so you see the closest threat without opening anything.
  • Flags a spot as open field when there's no bowl competition within a mile and a half — that's a green light.
  • Flip any of these on and off as pins on the map to size up a whole market at a glance.
Read the corner, not just the map

Traffic counts on every site

  • Each site now shows its road's daily traffic count — real vehicles-per-day, pulled from public traffic data, right in the overview.
  • Tells you how a corner stacks up against your other candidates — "quieter than most," "busier than most" — at a glance.
  • It's context, not a score: a fast-casual bowl spot doesn't need a freeway, so we surface it for you to weigh — it never bumps a site's fit up or down on its own.
See it before you drive it

Walk any site from your phone

  • One-click Street View on any site — stand right on the corner without leaving the couch.
  • Satellite / aerial view — flip the whole map from streets to overhead and eyeball the parking lot, patio room, and how you'd get in and out.
  • Works on every site, Phoenix and Austin.
