How this works

This page talks straight to the dashboard now — no copying commands, nothing to send to Skip. Add a city, or add a franchisee, and it shows up right away.

  1. Adding a person? Share their Google Sheet with us first (one email, below). We check that we can actually read it — and that it's laid out right — before anything gets added, so a bad link never blanks out their part of the map.
  2. Fill in the form and hit the button.
  3. Made a mistake? Anything you add can be removed, and anything removed can be undone for a few minutes right after.
